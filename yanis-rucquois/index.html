Founder

Yanis Rucquois

Yanis Rucquois is a Belgian macro trader and the founder of MAISON YR, a trading education house built on what actually moves markets: the macro first, the chart last. He trained as a bank analyst preparing the data, the news and the fundamentals for the trading desk, then traded the bank's capital before going independent. Before trading, he was a footballer. Every trade he takes on the desk is posted before entry, and the record is public.

What he does

Yanis teaches macro trading the way a bank desk reads the market: central banks, rates, flows and the news that moves them, then risk, the trading journal and the discipline that most retail courses skip. Every trade risks 1% of the account.

MAISON YR does not sell signals. The goal is that members learn to read the market and take their own trades, and end up needing no one, not even him.

The public record

Every trade counts only if it was posted before entry or called live with members watching. Losses and breakevens stay on the record. Each month is reviewed in the open. The current record is on the home page.

Football first

Football came before all of it. Yanis played from the age of three, left home at twelve for the game, and played in Belgium, the Netherlands, France, Greece, England and the United States, until injuries and five surgeries ended his career. He had found trading at sixteen. When football stopped, it became the whole job.
