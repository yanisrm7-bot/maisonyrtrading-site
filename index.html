The Philosophy

Most retail traders are learning a version of trading that was never designed to make them successful.

They learn how to react to price — but never how to understand it. At MAISON YR, trading is approached through macroeconomics, institutional positioning, liquidity, psychology and structured execution, because long-term consistency comes from understanding how markets truly operate.

01

Most traders never learn what actually moves price

Price is driven by macroeconomics, liquidity, institutional positioning, interest rates and capital flows — not isolated patterns or random signals. The MAISON YR framework was built around understanding those deeper market dynamics first.

02

Technical analysis alone is not enough

Charts matter. But most retail traders learn technical analysis in isolation — patterns, indicators, setups — never how to connect them to the broader financial environment driving price underneath the surface. Here, technical execution is combined with macro, liquidity and structured reasoning.

03

Institutions leave footprints in the market

Banks, hedge funds and institutional participants cannot move massive amounts of capital invisibly. Their positioning impacts liquidity, volatility and long-term direction — leaving measurable footprints. Members learn to identify and understand those flows instead of emotionally reacting against them.

04

Consistency comes from structure

The biggest difference between profitable and unprofitable traders is rarely intelligence. It is structure. Risk management, emotional discipline, controlled execution and consistency under pressure separate long-term traders from short-term gamblers.

05

The goal is independence

Markets evolve. Conditions change. Narratives rotate. Traders who rely on external signals struggle the moment the environment shifts. MAISON YR develops traders capable of understanding markets independently — reasoning, structure, adaptability. The objective is not dependency. The objective is understanding.

The Story

Football. Banking. Markets. MAISON YR.

The Story — From the Pitch to the Desk ~2 min · Football. Banking. Markets. MAISON YR. Landing soon

I started football at three years old. I left my parents' house at twelve to pursue it, and turned professional at fifteen. The game took me across the world — France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Greece, the United States.

I started trading at sixteen, while still playing. I joined a trading formation in Belgium — in person, a full year. It was technical analysis only: charts, patterns, indicators. After that year, my account was up and down — mostly down. That failure taught me the most important lesson of my career: charts don't move markets. The macro does. Every market is correlated — stocks, forex, gold, oil, crypto — and there is always a reason price moves. The course never taught me the reason.

So I went to find it where it actually lives. I studied economics, earned an internship at a bank, and became an analyst — the person who reads everything impacting markets and feeds it up the chain to the traders. Then I became a trader for the bank. Then I left to trade my own capital.

Since then, trading has changed my life. I've generated over $5M in trading profits — and I've never touched that capital. Not once. The math is simple: 50% on $10M is $5M. 50% on $1M is $500K. Same performance, different outcome. My capital compounds toward one goal — generational wealth — and my lifestyle is funded elsewhere. Never from the trading account.

In 2023, still playing professionally, I was diagnosed with arthritis in my ankle. Doctors told me to stop. I didn't listen. Four surgeries later, the ankle still hurts, the arthritis isn't leaving, and the level I played at isn't coming back. Last year, I retired. Football had been my life since I was three years old. Losing it left a hole.

Then the #1 trading app on the App Store reached out and asked me to become a mentor. I started sharing my trades there — and understood something quickly: giving people trades will never make them independent. Signals don't build skill. What builds skill is the reality of trading — how banks trade, how institutions trade: fundamentals first, macro first, knowing WHY price moves before you ever touch an entry. That became the new passion.

MAISON YR launched in April 2026 with seven members — and then I closed the door. Not because it wasn't working: the quality was there from day one. But there was no structure to scale it yet. So for months, behind the scenes, I worked with those seven one-on-one, around the clock, while building the platform that could carry the standard without me standing over it.

Now it can — and the door is open. The goal has never changed: make you financially free and independent in your trading. Teach you to fish. That's the house.

— Yanis Rucquois · Founder, MAISON YR

Common Questions

This is not built for most traders.

MAISON YR was built for people who want to understand markets professionally — not consume random signals and hope for the best.

01What actually makes MAISON YR different?+

MAISON YR is not built as a content business. It is built as a professional trading ecosystem. Every part is connected: the Course, the live sessions, the macro reports, the trade updates, the psychology work, the community structure and the execution process. Nothing is random. Nothing is based on hype. The objective is to develop traders capable of thinking independently in real market conditions — not traders dependent on someone else forever.

The Complete Institutional Program will sell separately at $4,000 when complete — while it releases, active Floor members receive every chapter, included. Cancel anytime. No call required to join.

03Is MAISON YR beginner-friendly?+

Yes — but it is not beginner-minded. The program starts from the foundations: market structure, execution, TradingView setup, risk management and macro basics. From the beginning, members are progressively introduced to macroeconomics, institutional positioning, liquidity, central-bank policy, execution discipline and the psychology behind professional decision making. Complete beginners follow the curriculum step by step; advanced traders usually realize quickly they were missing the structural side of markets entirely. New members start with "Your First 7 Days" — an exact roadmap — and two clear paths depending on whether you're brand new or already trading.

04Why do most retail traders fail?+

Because they learn trading backwards. Most retail education focuses on indicators, patterns, entries and social-media setups. But price is moved by central-bank policy, liquidity flows, institutional positioning, interest-rate expectations, macroeconomic shifts and large capital-allocation decisions. Without understanding those forces, traders spend years reacting to price instead of understanding why it moves. MAISON YR was built specifically to solve that problem.

05Is this a signal service?+

No. Trades are shared live with full transparency, but the objective is education and understanding — not blind copying. Every position includes macro reasoning, technical structure, risk management, execution logic, trade management and ongoing updates in real time. Members don't just see what is being traded — they understand why the position exists, what invalidates it, what confirms it, and how professional risk management is applied around it. Over time, members naturally develop their own analysis and execution process. That is the goal.

06What do members actually get every week?+

Daily market briefings before sessions open · every trade posted to the desk before entry, with full reasoning and live management · a minimum of two live Zoom sessions per week — often more — plus the Sunday Market Room · weekly institutional-style macro research reports · monthly performance reviews with the full report card · chaptered session replays and archives · psychology and execution discussions · private community access. The environment is active every week — not a static course library.

07How important is psychology inside the program?+

Extremely. Most traders don't fail because they lack a strategy — they fail because they can't execute consistently, can't manage emotions, overleverage, revenge trade, panic in drawdowns or abandon discipline under pressure. Psychology is integrated directly into the framework: execution discipline work, emotional control, cognitive-bias awareness, journaling structure, routine development, and intervention from a stress-management specialist focused on performance under pressure. Trading is not only analytical. It is behavioral.

08How much time do I realistically need?+

Less than you think — and you should keep your job. Inside, "The Floor Around Your Job" gives you honest time-budget plans: 20 minutes a day (the morning briefing and the desk check) · 1 hour a day (briefing, desk, and part of a session or replay) · weekends only (the Sunday Market Room, the weekly report, and chaptered replays). Positions are held for days, alerts do the watching, and every session is recorded. The process adapts to your life, not the other way around. The important part is consistency — not speed.

09What happens during the fit check?+

The fit check exists to make sure the fit is correct on both sides. It's not a high-pressure sales call — it's 30 minutes to see where you are and whether this is your environment: your current level, your goals, your experience, your mindset, and what you're actually looking for from trading. From there we determine whether MAISON YR is the right fit, which program makes sense, and your best starting point. The objective is long-term alignment — not forcing people into something that doesn't fit.

10Who is MAISON YR NOT for?+

People looking for overnight success, gambling, unrealistic promises, "100% win rate" systems or effortless money will not fit this environment. MAISON YR is built for people willing to learn seriously, think independently, stay disciplined and develop real long-term skill in the markets. That standard is what protects the quality of the ecosystem.
